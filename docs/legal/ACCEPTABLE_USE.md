# Chitin Acceptable Use

Version: DRAFT 0.1 (2026-10-11).

This page says what you must not do with Chitin. It is part of the Terms of Use (`TERMS.md`). It
applies to everyone: people who use Chitin, people who run copies of it, and people who run a relay.
It is meant to be read, so it is short and plain.

The idea behind it: Chitin is a browser. A browser can be used for almost anything, good and bad. We
want it used for good things, and we are not able to watch what you do. So you are the one in charge of
following these rules, and you are responsible if you do not.

## Do not use Chitin to

1. **Break the law.** Do nothing that is illegal where you are, where the other person is, or where the
   servers are.
2. **Harm children.** Do not use Chitin to find, make, share, request or store sexual material involving
   anyone under 18, to groom or exploit a child, or to contact a child for harm. We will report what the
   law requires us to report. This is the one rule with no exceptions at all.
3. **Harass, threaten or stalk anyone.** That includes doxxing (publishing someone's private details),
   hate that targets people for who they are, incitement to violence, and unwanted contact meant to scare
   or hurt.
4. **Spread malware or attack computers.** No viruses, ransomware, spyware, botnets or phishing kits. No
   scanning other people's networks or ports, no brute-forcing logins, no exploiting vulnerabilities in systems
   you do not own or have written permission to test, no denial-of-service attacks, no using Chitin or a
   relay as a springboard for any of these. (If you do real security research with permission, keep to
   the scope you have been given.)
5. **Commit fraud.** Scams, phishing, impersonating a person or company to cheat someone, selling or using
   stolen cards, accounts, identities or data, money laundering, or evading sanctions.
6. **Share material that infringes copyright or other rights.** Do not use Chitin to download or share
   pirated works, to run piracy services, or to get around paid access you do not have. Respect
   other people's copyrights, trademarks and privacy.
7. **Hide wrongdoing from lawful monitoring.** Do not use Chitin to cover up criminal activity, to hide
   it from the police or courts acting lawfully, or to hide abuse of a child from the people responsible for
   them.
8. **Break the rules of the network you are on.** If a school, university, workplace, library or home
   network tells you that you may not use certain software or visit certain sites, do not use Chitin to
   do it. Chitin is not a tool for getting around such rules (see section 5 of the Terms).
9. **Cheat or disrupt others.** No spam, no fake accounts in bulk, no scraping that breaks a site's
   rules or overloads it, no cheating in exams or competitions, and no flooding or abusing services.
10. **Hurt other people who use Chitin, relays or Tor.** Do not use a relay to cause its operator to be blocked or
    shut down; do not attack Tor or other users on it.
11. **Pretend to be us.** Do not use the Chitin name or logo in a way that suggests your copy is the
    official one (see `TRADEMARKS.md`), and do not remove or fake the marks in the software that show
    where a copy came from.
12. **Help anyone do the things above.**

## Rules for people who share or run Chitin

- **If you publish a copy or fork,** you must follow the licence (the GPL: same licence, source offered,
  notices kept), rename and rebrand it, and must not describe it as unblocking school, work or other filters.
- **If you run a relay,** you must follow `relay-operators.md`: keep the safe defaults, have an abuse
  contact, and follow the law and your hosting provider's rules. You are responsible for the traffic that
  goes through your relay.
- **If you run a website with Chitin on it,** you are responsible for it. Do not collect data from
  children, and read `PRIVACY.md`.

## What happens if you break these rules

- Anything the project operates may be taken away from you straight away.
- Your licence may end (see the Terms and the licence).
- Relay operators may block you, and may report you where the law requires.
- We may report illegal activity to the authorities.

We cannot see what you do with Chitin and we do not try to. But if someone tells us about misuse of the
project, we will look at it.

## Questions you might have

- *Is it all right to use Chitin to keep websites from seeing my IP address?* Yes, if what you do on
  those websites is lawful. See the honest description of what Tor mode does in `TERMS.md` section 7.
- *Is it all right to use it at school or work?* Only if the rules there allow it. If you are not sure, ask.
- *Can I use it to get to a site my school blocks?* No. Do not use Chitin for that.
