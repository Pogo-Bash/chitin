## 7. No guarantee of anonymity or security

- **Chitin does not make you anonymous.** Nobody should rely on it for anonymity, and we do not say it
  provides any.
- **Tor mode** ("Route through Tor") sends your traffic through the Tor network. That hides your IP
  address from the websites you visit. It does not make you anonymous. Websites can still recognise you
  from other things: your login, your cookies, how your browser behaves, and how it looks to the site.
  Chitin is not the Tor Browser. If you need strong anonymity, use the Tor Browser and read the Tor
  Project's guidance. Tor mode is also not a way to hide what you do from your school, employer or
  anyone who monitors the network you are on; they may still see that you are using Tor or Chitin.
- **Relays can see some things** (section 8). Chitin does the encryption of your connections to websites
  inside the browser, so a relay should not be able to read the content of secure connections, but it can see who
  is connecting and where to.
- **Sandboxing is not perfect.** Chitin separates sites from each other, but software has bugs.
  We do not promise that any site cannot harm your device, data or accounts.
- **Do not rely on Chitin for safety-critical or high-risk situations** (for example, where being
  identified could put you in danger).
