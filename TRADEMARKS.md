# Chitin trademark policy

Version: DRAFT 0.1 (2026-10-11).

The GPL in `LICENSE.md` (version 2 or any later version) covers Chitin's code. It does not give you any right to use
Chitin's name or logo. This policy says how the name and logo may be used.

The point of this policy is simple: when you see "Chitin", you should be able to trust that it is
the real, official project, run by its author, and not a copy made by someone else, perhaps with
advertising around it or with different goals. Trademark law protects that trust. It is not meant to
stop honest people from talking about Chitin or building on it.

## 2. What you may do without asking

- **Say what it is,** accurately: "Chitin is a browser that runs inside a web page", "this works with Chitin",
  "I wrote a guide for Chitin". Plain, truthful references (including in reviews, articles, tutorials
  and research papers) are fine.
- **Link to the official project** and use the name as the name of the project when you do.
- **Distribute unmodified copies** of the official software under the licence, with the name intact, and with
  the notices the licence requires.
- **Use the name in your own project's documentation** to say that it is compatible with Chitin, in a way
  that makes clear it is not Chitin itself. For example, "Compatible with Chitin. Not affiliated with
  or endorsed by Chitin." Use plain text, not the logo.
- **Run an unmodified copy for yourself** (on your own machine or private network).
  If you make it public, section 4 applies.

## 3. What you may not do

- **Use the name or logo for a modified version.** If you change Chitin in any way (code, build, bundled
  list, branding, network settings), you may not call it Chitin or use the logo or anything confusingly similar.
- **Present your copy, site, relay, app, package or account as the official one,** or suggest the project
  made, runs, supports, endorses or is partnered with it, when that is not true.
- **Use Chitin in a domain name, subdomain, app name, package name, social-media handle, channel or bot
  name** without written permission, even if you say you are "unofficial". Examples that are not allowed:
  `chitin-unblocked.example`, `@chitin_official`, `chitinproxy.app`, "Chitin Games".
- **Pair the name with the kind of site we exist to be different from:** "unblocked", "proxy", "games",
  "school", "bypass", "free VPN", and similar. Even a truthful use is not allowed next to these words.
- **Use the logo** in any altered form (changed colours, proportions or elements) or as part of your own logo.
- **Put advertising around Chitin's name or an unmodified copy of it,** or sell, rent or take payment for access
  to it, under the name Chitin. (The GPL lets you charge for copies and services under your own name; it
  gives no right to the name.)
- **Remove or imitate the marks in the software** that show where a copy came from. Doing so is also treated
  as a licence matter (`takedown.md`).
- **Register or use Chitin** as a trademark, company name or domain name, in any country.

## 4. Forks and copies: rename and rebrand

If you publish your own version or copy:

1. **Choose your own name and logo** that is clearly different from Chitin's. Do not start with "Chitin" or
   include it.
2. **Rebrand:** replace the name, logos, icons and colour schemes that identify the official product, in the
   user interface, the title bar, the about page, package metadata, documentation, store listings,
   screenshots and domain names.
3. **Keep the notices the licence and the other licences require:** the copyright and `Required Notice:` lines
   in each file, the GPL text, the Ladybird BSD-2-Clause notice and the other credits in `CREDITS.md`. Credit is honest and encouraged:
   "Based on Chitin by [OWNER NAME]" in plain text is fine, and is encouraged.
4. **Say it is unofficial** and not endorsed by the project, on the about page and the site.
5. **Follow the licence.** In particular: keep it under the GPL, and give everyone you distribute it to the
   corresponding source (or a written offer of it), the licence text and the notices.
6. **Do not describe the fork as a school, work or firewall unblocker** or a way to evade monitoring.
7. **Do not use the official origin or anything confusingly similar** for your site.

If your fork cannot honestly follow these rules, do not publish it.

## 7. Reporting misuse of the Chitin name

If you see a site, app, package, account or relay using the name or logo without permission, or
presenting itself as Chitin, or something carrying the name that looks like a scam, an ad farm or a
school/firewall unblocker, tell us at [LEGAL EMAIL] with:

- the address (URL) or location, a screenshot or archive link, and the date you saw it;
- what you think is wrong;
- your name and contact details (we will not share them without your agreement unless the law
  requires it).

We will look at it, and if it is a problem we may contact the owner and the host. See `takedown.md`.

To report misuse of someone else's name that you found in or around Chitin (for example Tor's), tell
the owner of that name. The Tor Project's address for this is tor-brand@rt.torproject.org.

## 8. Permission

dishonest, but it can end permission going forward.
